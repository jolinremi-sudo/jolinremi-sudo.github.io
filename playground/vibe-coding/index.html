PLAYGROUND / 02VIBE CODING2026

FROM TASTE
TO A WORKING
WEBSITE.

I started with references, a feeling and a rough idea of how I wanted the portfolio to behave — then used AI-assisted coding to turn that direction into a working website.

我不是从代码开始，
而是从视觉参考、内容结构和“我希望它给人什么感觉”开始。
再借助 AI 辅助编程，把这些判断一步步变成真正可以浏览的网站。

AI-ASSISTED BUILD /
HUMAN-DIRECTED

01 / COLLECTING CLUES

I WASN'T
LOOKING FOR
A TEMPLATE.
I WAS
COLLECTING
CLUES.

I was drawn to different things in different references — playful color, editorial typography, folder-like structures, paper textures and small imperfect details.

我并不是想找到一个网站直接照着做。
真正吸引我的，是不同参考里各自成立的部分：
配色、字体层级、纸张感、档案结构，以及一点不那么规整的手作感。

02 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

TASTE NEEDS
TRANSLATION.

A reference only tells me what I like. The next step was understanding why — and turning those observations into repeatable design decisions.

“喜欢”本身还不能成为设计方向。
我需要继续拆解：
到底是颜色、字体、结构，还是某种不那么规整的视觉节奏吸引了我，
再把这些感觉变成可以反复使用的规则。

03 / BUILD & ITERATE

THE FIRST
ANSWER WAS
RARELY THE
FINAL ONE.

AI made it faster to turn decisions into something visible. But seeing the page made new problems visible too — hierarchy, image scale, copy, navigation and how real content changed the composition.

AI 让我更快看到一个想法真正落到页面上的样子，
但页面出现以后，新的判断才开始：
图片够不够有力量？信息层级是否清楚？
真实内容放进去以后，原来的版式还成立吗？
哪些东西应该留下，哪些应该删掉？

STRUCTURE→REAL CONTENT→REVIEW→REFINE

04 / THE RESULT

ONE SYSTEM.
DIFFERENT
STORIES.

The final portfolio needed to hold commercial work, visual storytelling and personal experiments without making them feel like separate identities.

最后我想解决的并不只是“做一个好看的网页”。

它需要同时容纳商业项目、消费者洞察、视觉内容和个人实验，
但依然让人感觉它们来自同一个人、同一套视觉语言。

CONTENT × BRAND × VISUAL × AI-ASSISTED MAKING

05 / REFLECTION

AI CAN MAKE
THINGS FASTER.
DIRECTION
STILL NEEDS
A HUMAN.

The tool helped me make it.
The decisions made it mine.

This project changed how I think about making digital work.

I don't need to begin with perfect technical knowledge to test an idea. But I do need to know what I am trying to communicate, what feels right, what feels generic, and when something needs another pass.

AI shortened the distance between an idea and a working result. It did not remove the need for taste, structure or judgment.

这个项目让我重新理解了“把想法做出来”这件事。

我不需要先成为专业开发者，才能验证一个数字产品的想法。
但我仍然需要判断：
我到底想表达什么，
什么是适合这个项目的，
什么看起来过于模板化，
以及什么时候还需要再改一次。

AI 缩短了想法与成品之间的距离，
但没有替代审美、结构和判断。
